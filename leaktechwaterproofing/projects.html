A few of the problems we solve

Residential roof leak

Identified the source of a persistent roof leak, applied waterproofing membrane and completed repairs to stop water ingress and protect the ceiling below.

Bathroom waterproofing

Full bathroom and shower waterproofing before tiling — preventing moisture penetration and long-term damage to walls and floors.

Exterior repaint

Surface preparation, crack repairs and a full exterior repaint that refreshed the property and added a protective, weather-resistant finish.

Balcony terrace sealing

Waterproofed a balcony terrace to prevent leaks into the structure below, with a durable, low-maintenance finish.

Rising damp treatment

Diagnosed rising damp on an exterior wall and applied damp-proofing treatment to arrest moisture and protect the masonry.

Commercial roof maintenance

Routine roof maintenance, waterproofing and painting for a commercial building to extend roof life and prevent leaks.

Company overview

Protecting and improving properties

LeakTech Waterproofing is a professional construction and maintenance company specialising in waterproofing, painting and roofing solutions.

We are committed to providing reliable workmanship, quality materials, professional service and practical solutions that protect and improve residential and commercial properties.
